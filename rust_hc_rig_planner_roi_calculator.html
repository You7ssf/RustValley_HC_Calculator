Rust HC Rig Architecture & Mechanics

The Rust Hardware Crypto (HC) mining plugin replicates crypto currency mining rigs in-game. To assemble operational rigs, players must adhere to socket hierarchy, electrical load constraints, and thermal dissipation metrics.

1. Motherboard Socket Rails

The primary motherboard determines which expansion slots are electrically enabled:

  • Tier 3 MB: Unlocks slots with minimum requirement of T3 (1x CPU, 1x Cooler, 1x PSU, 2x GPU, 2x RAM).
  • Tier 4 MB: Unlocks all T3 slots plus 1 additional GPU slot and 2 additional RAM slots.
  • Tier 5 MB: Unlocks the entire 18-slot chassis including dual-CPUs, dual-PSUs, 4 GPUs, and 8 RAM modules.

2. Power & PSU Overload

Every installed component draws wattage (W). Power supplies furnish capacity:

  • T3PS: 800 Watts output.
  • T4PS: 1200 Watts output.
  • T5PS: 2000 Watts output.

If total power demand exceeds cumulative PSU capacity, the rig transitions to PSU Overload and will fail.

3. Mathematical Formulas

# Gross Daily Rate Daily_Yield = Σ (Component_Yield)
# Amortization Payback Period Payback_Days = Total_Cost_HC / Daily_Yield_HC
# Wipe Net Income Forecast Net_Profit = (Daily_Yield × (Wipe_Hours / 24)) - Total_Cost_HC
